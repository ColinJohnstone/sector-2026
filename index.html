The SecTor 2026 thesis

AI isn't creating an entirely new class of cybersecurity problems. It's compressing the time, cost and expertise required to exploit the ones we already have.

How we got here

Same targets, faster attacks

The techniques didn't change much. Each step made them faster, cheaper and more automated.

  1. 2010Stuxnet

    Two governments, an estimated $300M and four zero-days to reach a Siemens S7 controller.

    Day 1 keynote abstract
  2. 2010sCloud automation

    Infrastructure becomes an API. Environments become scriptable, and so do attacks against them.

  3. 2022LLMs go mainstream

    Writing code and text becomes a commodity anyone can access.

  4. 2025AI-assisted attacks

    Threat intelligence teams document state-backed actors using LLMs in their operations.

    Google Threat Intelligence
  5. 2026Autonomous agents

    AI-written scripts hit the same S7 PLCs, and agent-driven cloud chains run in minutes in SecTor demos.

    CISA AA26-231A
