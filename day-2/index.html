    What I'm taking away

    Six things to remember

    Local models help attackers live off the land

    They're already installed, trusted and able to read files and write code. Attackers don't need to bring their own tools.

    Scope what local AI can touch

    Zero trust for models: only the files they're working on right now, and remove them where they aren't needed.

    Finding is cheap, proving is expensive

    AI can produce thousands of findings. Verification is the bottleneck, and plausibility isn't proof.

    Buy the hunter, own the verifier

    Discovery tools are a commodity. The step that proves a finding is where our own context matters.

    Hardware isn't a safe boundary

    Bit flips in GPU memory reached root on the host. Shared GPUs, ECC and driver patching deserve attention.

    Spyware is aimed at people

    Journalists, lawyers and activists are prime targets, and public-interest research is often the first warning everyone else gets.

    AI is unpredictable. The proof isn't. We check the proof, not the AI.

    Rahul Jaisinghani, BrowserStack
