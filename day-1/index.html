The brief

If you only read one thing

AI security is turning out to be less a new category of security and more what happens when existing cloud and security problems start happening at machine speed.

Speed

Minutes, not days

Reconnaissance, privilege escalation and data access that took hours or days can now run end to end in minutes.

Identity

Agents nobody tracks

We're deploying more agents without a mature way to inventory, identify and govern them.

Fundamentals

Basics matter more

Segmentation, least privilege, isolation, logging and blast-radius reduction are possibly more important than ever.

    What I'm taking away

    Six things to remember

    Speed is the real change

    AI doesn't invent every technique. It makes existing ones faster, cheaper and easier to chain together.

    Fundamentals matter more

    Segmentation, identity, least privilege, isolation and smaller blast radius kept coming up because AI makes weak architecture easier to exploit.

    Agents need identity

    With thousands or millions of agents, we need to know what they are, what they can access and who is responsible for them.

    Deception gets interesting

    Canaries and decoys work better when the attacker is autonomous and explores everything.

    Security economics are changing

    Falling model costs make continuous testing realistic, for attackers and defenders alike.

    Governance can't be an afterthought

    The technology is moving faster than ownership models, and that gap becomes a security problem.

    The day wasn't about “AI security” as a separate thing. It was about cloud, identity and software security operating at AI speed.
